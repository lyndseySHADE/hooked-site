Questions

FAQ

How long will my bag take?

If the colours you'd like are already in stock, I can have your bag ready in 5 working days. For a fully customised design, turnaround varies, so DM me on Instagram and we'll work out a timeline together.

Can I choose any colours?

Yes! Describe your dream combination in the order form, or send inspiration photos, and I'll match it as closely as possible using premium T-shirt yarn.

Can I send inspiration photos?

Absolutely, just upload them in the order form. Pinterest screenshots, outfit photos or a favourite sunset all help me get your bag right.

Do you ship across the UK?

Yes, UK shipping is available at checkout. Your bag is carefully packaged and tracked.

Can I collect locally?

Yes, choose Collection at checkout and I'll be in touch to arrange a time that suits you.
