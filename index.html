Applying for P1

Key dates for September 2027

P1 applications are made online through the Education Authority (EA). Every parent must apply, even if your child attends a nursery unit.

  1. 9 December 2026

    Primary school admissions criteria published on the EA website.

  2. 6 January 2027

    Online applications open on EA Connect at 12 noon.

  3. 20 January 2027

    Applications close at 12 noon. Please don't miss the deadline.

  4. 18 March 2027

    Outcomes issued to parents by email or letter.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book for the open sessions?

You don't have to, as everyone is very welcome, but it really helps us to know roughly how many families to expect. Please RSVP to help us facilitate numbers.

Can my child come with me?

Yes, please bring them! Both events and individual appointments are for prospective P1 children and their parents/carers. For the Big Bedtime Read, they can even come in their pyjamas with their favourite teddy.

Is there after-school care?

Yes. Our After School Club is based in the school's Community Room and runs 2pm–5pm during term time, with snacks included. Places are booked in advance through ParentPay. See prices and details.

Is there a breakfast club?

Confirm, or remove this question.

When do I apply for a P1 place?

Applications open on EA Connect at 12 noon on 6 January 2027 and close at 12 noon on 20 January 2027. See all key dates.
